Our Firm

Built on Expertise,
Guided by Integrity

Tax Star Partners is a state and local tax advisory firm serving multistate businesses. Our work concentrates on refund recovery and state tax planning — identifying overpayments in previously filed returns and pursuing them through amended filings and refund claims, then applying what those reviews surface to a client's forward-looking positions. Engagements typically involve nexus determination, apportionment methodology, and revenue sourcing analysis, with particular depth in industries where receipts don't sort neatly under market-based sourcing rules. We are not a compliance shop; we are brought in where the technical position drives the number.

Tax Star Partners was founded on a simple belief: every client deserves a dedicated advocate who understands the full complexity of the state and local tax landscape. We bring together deep technical expertise and personalized service to deliver results that make a measurable difference.

From nexus determinations to multi-state restructuring, we work alongside CFOs, controllers, and business owners to identify risks, capture opportunities, and build defensible positions — all without losing sight of your broader business goals.

Comprehensive State Tax Services

State Income Tax

Multi-state income apportionment, nexus studies, and compliance reviews for corporations and pass-through entities operating across state lines.

Sales & Use Tax

Voluntary disclosure agreements, nexus evaluations, audit defense, and transaction-level review to minimize sales tax exposure and liability.

Nexus & Apportionment

Determine your physical and economic nexus footprint, restructure apportionment factors, and develop state-by-state tax minimization strategies.

Tax Credits & Incentives

Identify and maximize R&D credits, investment incentives, enterprise zone benefits, and other state-level tax credit opportunities for your business.

Audit Defense

Experienced representation before state revenue departments. We manage correspondence, document requests, protests, and appeals to protect your interests.

Voluntary Disclosure

Proactively resolve unfiled state tax obligations through voluntary disclosure programs to limit lookback periods and eliminate penalties.
